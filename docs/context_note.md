# Context Note

**Who it is for:** Vidya is designed for self-taught AI/ML learners who struggle to diagnose genuine mastery versus superficial familiarity when learning complex concepts like Neural Networks, Transformers, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

**Where data came from:** The curriculum graph is structured from canonical deep learning foundations (Goodfellow et al., Vaswani et al., modern RAG reference architectures). Question items and concept nodes are structured and verified with human review.

**Objective parts covered:** Vidya couples a mathematically rigorous Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) engine with prerequisite-gated sequencing. The LLM acts solely as a communicator (re-explaining in alternate modalities such as analogies or worked examples upon struggle), while the probabilistic model maintains the objective ground truth of learner knowledge.
